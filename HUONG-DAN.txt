APP BAO GIA HOANG MINH WCAR - HUONG DAN DUA LEN MANG VA CAI VAO MAY

BUOC 1. Dua len mang (lam 1 lan, mien phi)
 - Giai nen file zip, ra thu muc "bao-gia-hm".
 - Vao https://app.netlify.com/drop (dang ky tai khoan mien phi neu duoc yeu cau).
 - Keo ca thu muc "bao-gia-hm" tha vao trang. Netlify cho ban 1 duong dan dang https://ten-gi-do.netlify.app
 - Dang nhap tai khoan de giu duong dan lau dai. Co the doi ten duong dan trong Site settings.

BUOC 2. Cai len iPhone
 - Mo duong dan bang Safari -> nut Chia se -> "Them vao Man hinh chinh" -> Them.
 - Tu nay chi mo app bang bieu tuong tren man hinh chinh (du lieu cua app tren man hinh chinh tach rieng voi Safari).

BUOC 3. Cai len may tinh
 - Mo duong dan bang Chrome hoac Edge -> bam bieu tuong "Cai dat" o cuoi thanh dia chi (hoac menu -> Cai dat Bao gia HM...).
 - Chon tao loi tat o Desktop / ghim vao thanh taskbar.

LUU Y
 - Du lieu (khach, bao gia, bang gia, cai dat) luu rieng tren tung thiet bi. Vao Cai dat -> "Sao luu du lieu" de tai file sao luu, va "Khoi phuc tu file" de chuyen sang thiet bi khac.
 - Sau lan mo dau tien, app chay duoc ca khi mat mang.
 - Khi can cap nhat app, tha lai thu muc moi len Netlify (muc Deploys).
